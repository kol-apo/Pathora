About Taiwo

Taiwo is a Senior Product Manager at Google, where he leads a cross-functional team building developer tools used by engineers across West Africa. Before Google, he spent four years at Flutterwave helping define the product strategy for their business payments suite.

Originally from Lagos, Taiwo studied Computer Science at the University of Ibadan before moving into product management — a path he had to figure out largely on his own. He's now passionate about helping African students take that same journey with less friction and more clarity.
